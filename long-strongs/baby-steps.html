Long Strong Baby Step 1
Macro Minimums
Get the right macros for strength and gut health.

This is where everyone starts. Before cardio, before the gym, before any of it — you need to feed your body correctly. Your body cannibalizes muscle if you don't hit your protein. Glucose spikes when you skip fiber. You can't out-train a bad foundation.

Hit these minimums every day for two weeks before moving to Step 2. Protein first, then fiber, then everything else.

"You are someone who feeds their body. Protein first. Every meal, every time."
  • Prioritize protein-rich foods first at every meal, fiber-rich foods second
  • Hit the big 4 fists of protein per day
  • Your body eats your muscles if you don't feed it protein
  • Protein reduces glucose spikes — this is the foundation
  • Do this for 2 weeks consistently before moving to Step 2

Long Strong Baby Step 2
Do an Activity
Sign up for something fun, social, and cardio-related.

Not a solo treadmill in your basement. A run club. Dance cardio. Yoga. Tennis. Roller blading. The point is social pressure — because accountability you'd be embarrassed to skip is a feature, not a workaround.

We're aiming for Zone 2 cardio — the pace where you can hold a conversation but wouldn't want to. At least twice a week, aim for three.

"You live an active lifestyle. You are an active person."
  • Run club, dance cardio, yoga, cycle class, tennis, hockey, roller blading, MixxedFit
  • Zone 2 cardio = conversational pace, slightly labored breathing
  • Social pressure is accountability you'll actually use
  • Bring your kids. Walk your dog. Find every excuse to move.
  • Minimum 2× per week — aim for 3

Long Strong Baby Step 3
Strength Mindset
Go to the gym 2× a week. For month one, just go and leave.

This step isn't about performance. It's about identity. For the entire first month, you don't even have to work out — you just have to show up. Put on gym clothes. Walk in. Leave. That's it. You're building a self-concept: I am someone who goes to the gym.

If you work out at home: put on gym clothes and stand next to your spot. Same principle. The behavior creates the belief.

"You are someone who goes to the gym. You are strong."
  • Month one: show up and leave — no workout required
  • Regular gym, CrossFit, calisthenics, home weights — find what you'll return to
  • Get gym clothes you actually feel good in
  • At home: put on gym clothes and stand next to your spot 2× a week
  • This is about building identity, not performance

Long Strong Baby Step 4
Measure Progress
Pick a goal. Get a diagnostic tool. Read the trend.

Steps 1–3 are locked in. Now it's time to understand where you actually are. Pick one goal — body composition, strength, metabolic health — and get a tool that measures it accurately over time.

Don't obsess over single readings. Tools can be inaccurate and your body fluctuates. The trend over 4–8 weeks is what matters. Plateaus happen to everyone — they are not a signal to stop.

"You are someone who knows their numbers and trusts the process."
  • Body composition scan (muscle mass to fat ratio) — most useful tool
  • Progress photos — weekly, same time, same light, same angle
  • Tools can be inaccurate — trend over time matters more than single readings
  • Plateaus happen. They are not failure. They are the plateau before the breakthrough.

Long Strong Baby Step 5
Gain Excess Muscle
Focus on resistance training to reach your ideal muscle mass.

Muscle is your metabolic engine. The more you have, the more carbs your body soaks up through movement and insulin — which means better blood sugar, better energy, and better longevity. You're not just trying to look stronger. You're building the machine that lets you age differently than everyone around you.

The biggest physical struggle in old age is falling. Falling leads to fractures, which lead to surgeries, which lead to decline. Your muscle mass right now is your insurance policy. Build your emergency fund of muscle.

"You are someone who is strong. Your body is your most important long-term asset."
  • Resistance training 2–3× per week — maintain with 30 min, 2× a week minimum
  • Every time you're sore, muscle tissue was torn — fill it back in with protein
  • Muscles soak up carbs through movement or insulin — build them to metabolize better
  • The biggest struggle in old age is falling — build your emergency fund of muscle now
  • Functional fitness goal: tie your shoe standing on one foot

6
Long Strong Baby Step 6
Become Metabolically Healthy
Create a caloric imbalance until you reach your ideal body fat.

You have the muscle. Now it's time to burn the fat. Either increase your exercise load, decrease your carbs, or both — until you reach your ideal body fat percentage. This is the step where the Sick Care System loses its grip on you.

Being metabolically healthy means reduced inflammation throughout your body, better hormones, better brain function, and a dramatically lower risk of the chronic diseases the Sick Care System profits from treating. This is the goal the entire framework is built toward.

"You are metabolically healthy. The Sick Care System does not profit from your body."
  • Create a caloric imbalance: increase exercise, decrease carbs, or both
  • Wrangle carbs and fats back to maintenance once goal is reached
  • This reduces inflammation throughout the entire body
  • Fat cells grow vs. multiply — once you've burned them, they can come back smaller
  • Goal marker: glucose test — pass it and you've arrived

"I am living long and strong. I'm around for it."
  • Hit your nutrient targets plus all supporting pillars — consistently
