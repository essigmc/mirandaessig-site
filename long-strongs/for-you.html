The Long Strongs Podcast

This Show Is Not
for Fitness People.

It's for people at a health inflection point — people who got the wake-up call and haven't decided to act yet. This podcast meets you at that exact moment.

Who This Is For

You Got the Wake-Up Call. You Just Haven't Acted Yet.

The Long Strongs exists for people at an inflection point. Not people who love working out. Not people who already have it figured out. People who are staring down a diagnosis, watching a parent decline, or feeling their own body starting to fail them — and who need someone to tell them, plainly and with certainty, what to do next.

If you already have a health coach, a meal plan, and a PR on your back squat, this show is not for you. But if you've been told to "eat better and exercise more" and still don't know where to start — you're exactly who this show is for.

The Callers

These Are the People Who Call In.

Newly Pre-Diabetic

The doctor flagged your A1C. You left the office with a pamphlet and no real instructions. You don't know if you need medication or just to change how you eat. You don't know where to start.

→ Likely on Step 1 or 2
Can't Keep Up With Your Kids

You're winded at the park. You said you'd start after the holidays. Then after school started. Then after the new year. The kids are getting faster and you're getting slower.

→ Likely on Step 2 or 3
Postpartum and Stuck

Your body changed and you don't recognize it. You've tried to get back on track a dozen times. Sleep-deprived and time-crunched, you need a plan that doesn't require hours you don't have.

→ Likely on Step 1
Watching a Parent Decline

Your mom has dementia. Your dad is on oxygen. You're watching what happens when the Sick Care System is all you have. You don't want that to be your kids' reality — and you don't know what to do about it.

→ Could be any step
Living With PCOS

You've been told your hormones make weight loss harder. Your doctor manages symptoms. Nobody is talking about the lifestyle changes that actually move the needle — and you're tired of not knowing.

→ Likely on Step 1 or 6
Started and Stopped 100 Times

You've done Whole30, Weight Watchers, Peloton, and three different gym memberships. Nothing stuck. You're not lazy — you've been given the wrong framework. This is a different framework.

→ Start at Step 1, in order

The Inflection Point

This show catches people at the exact moment when something has to change — and gives them a framework so clear they can't not act on it.

The goal isn't to get you motivated. Motivation fades. The goal is to give you a system so specific, so ordered, and so proven that motivation becomes irrelevant. You just do the next step.

This show IS

What You'll Get Here

  • ✓ A specific 7-step framework, applied to every caller
  • ✓ Declarative certainty — no hedging, no "it depends"
  • ✓ Real callers with real health problems and real names
  • ✓ The same answer, over and over, until it sticks
  • ✓ A host who was you — not someone who was always fit
  • ✓ A way to opt out of the Sick Care System for good

This show IS NOT

What You Won't Find Here

  • ✕ Biohacking for people already at 12% body fat
  • ✕ Supplement stacks, cold plunges, and optimization culture
  • ✕ Guest experts with conflicting opinions
  • ✕ "Everything in moderation" energy
  • ✕ Nuance that leaves you more confused than when you started
  • ✕ A host who hasn't been where you are

The Antagonist
