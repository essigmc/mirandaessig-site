What we're up against

A system that isn't built for your health

Food companies profit when you eat more, so the cheapest, tastiest food is built to keep you coming back. Hospitals are set up well to treat you once you get a major illness. Almost no one in the whole setup makes money when you're actually well.

It isn't that doctors don't care. They're stretched thin and burnt out, and they know lifestyle change is hard to prescribe and even harder to get people to take seriously. There's also no drug company paying them a cut to recommend a walk and more protein. Our health care simply wasn't designed for prevention. That's just not what it's built to do.

So if getting healthy has felt impossible, it isn't because you're weak. The whole thing is pointed the other way, and that's what we're here for. Think of us as your community. Call in anytime with your questions, and let's work toward a long, healthy life so you can be there for the people who count on you.

About the show

Science for the rest of us

It's a call-in show, so wherever you are with your health, we start right there. We're creating a consistent community dialog about health and answering your questions. Never thought about any of this? Perfect. Already deep in it? Great. No question is too basic, and you don't need to understand the world inside your body to pick up the phone.

A lot of great science is coming out on health and longevity, but most of it's buried in jargon. My job is to translate it, to turn the latest research into something simple you can actually use, for free. You shouldn't need a biology degree to take care of yourself.

I'm a chubby kid at heart who fell deep into the science of longevity and couldn't stop reading. I started and stopped a hundred times before any of it stuck, and that's exactly why I get it.

Do it for the people who count on you. Show up for yourself now so you can keep showing up for them. Come back each week, stay consistent, and remember you're not doing this alone. We're in this together.
