# Run your site locally (Windows)

You only do step 1 once.

1. **Install Node.js** — go to https://nodejs.org and click the big green **LTS** button. Run the installer and accept all the defaults.
2. **Double-click `start-dev.bat`** in this folder. The first time, it installs everything (a minute or two), then starts the site.
3. When the window shows **`localhost:4321`**, open that address in your browser. That's your site.

Leave the black window open while you work. As I edit files, your browser updates automatically. To stop, close that window.

If anything looks off, take a screenshot and send it to me.
